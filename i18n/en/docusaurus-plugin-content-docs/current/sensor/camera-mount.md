# Mount the cameras

Mount four CM-20 cameras at the front, rear, left, and right.

:::danger[Danger]
When working on the A pillar, do not intrude on the curtain airbag's deployment path. Secure the cable along the same route as the factory harness.
:::

## What you need

| Item | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Low-range torque wrench | 1 to 6 N·m |
| Degreaser | Isopropyl alcohol |
| Microfibre cloth | Lint free |

- **Conditions:** 2 people · about 60 minutes · mounting surface at **15 to 35 °C**
- **Before you start:** [3. Mount the LiDAR](./lidar-mount.md) complete

Adhesive cure time is **24 hours**, and 72 hours to fully cure. The cable connectors are FAKRA coaxial, colour coded by position.

Each camera cable connector carries a colour marking. Connecting the wrong one leaves the positions detected the wrong way round.

| Camera | Connector colour | Interface box port |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C1 front | Blue | `CAM1` |
| C2 rear | Green | `CAM2` |
| C3 left | Yellow | `CAM3` |
| C4 right | White | `CAM4` |

## C1 front camera

Mount it at the top centre of the windscreen, behind the rear-view mirror.

:::info[Caution]
Mounting it outside the wiper sweep leaves the view obscured in the rain. It is a common cause of repeated cleaning alerts.
:::

1. Clean the mounting position with degreaser and let it dry.
2. Peel the protective film from the bracket and attach it to the inside of the windscreen.
3. Push the camera into the bracket. A **click** means it is secured.
4. Confirm the camera's field of view is **inside** the wiper sweep.

| Check | Criterion |
| --- | --- |
| Distance from the top of the windscreen | 40 to 60 mm |
| Deviation from the vehicle centreline | ± 10 mm |
| Wiper sweep | The whole field of view is within it |
| Rear-view mirror interference | No contact at the mirror's maximum angle |

## C2 rear camera

Mount it at the top centre of the boot lid.

:::warning[Warning]
Too little cable slack at the hinge breaks the conductor after repeated opening and closing. There must be at least 30 mm of slack with the boot fully open.
:::

1. Clean the mounting position with degreaser.
2. Attach the bracket and fit the camera.
3. Pass the cable into the interior through the grommet in the boot lid hinge.
4. Open and close the boot **fully** and confirm the cable does not pull tight.

## C3 and C4 side cameras

Mount these under the left and right door mirrors. The procedure is the same on both sides.

1. Remove the cover on the underside of the door mirror.
2. Fix the camera bracket inside the mirror housing with two bolts, to **2.5 N·m**.
3. Adjust the camera lens to face 45° downwards.
4. Pass the cable into the door alongside the mirror harness.
5. Refit the cover.

| Check | Criterion |
| --- | --- |
| Lens angle | 45° downwards ± 5° |
| Door interference | No contact at full opening |
| Mirror folding | No cable interference when the mirror folds electrically |

On a vehicle with power folding mirrors, fold them five times and confirm the cable is not being pinched.

## Tidy the cables

1. Gather every camera cable at the interface box position in the boot.
2. Secure them to the factory harness with cable ties at **300 mm intervals**.
3. Form a drip loop near each connector.

:::info[Caution]
Do not pull a cable tight when securing it. Body vibration then passes straight into the connector.
:::

## Checks

| Check | Criterion |
| --- | --- |
| Connector colour matches the port | See the table |
| C1 wiper sweep | The whole field of view is within it |
| C2 hinge slack | At least 30 mm at full opening |
| C3 and C4 lens angle | 45° downwards ± 5° |
| Door and boot movement | No interference |
| Cable fixing interval | 300 mm or less |
| Lens cleanliness | No fingerprints or debris |
