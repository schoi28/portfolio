# Frequently asked questions

## Accounts and pairing

**Can I add several vehicles to one account?**

Yes. There is no limit on the number of vehicles. Switch between them at the top of the home screen.

**Can several people use one vehicle?**

Yes. The owner shares a key, as described in [Manage your digital key](./digital-key.md). One vehicle can have **5 digital keys, including the owner's**. Only one person owns the vehicle in the account, and only the owner can share and revoke keys.

**I have sold my vehicle. What do I do?**

Select **Settings > Vehicle > Unpair**. Unpairing revokes every shared digital key and deletes your driving history from the app. The previous owner has to unpair before a new owner can pair.

**I have changed my phone. What do I do?**

Sign in with the same account on the new phone and your vehicle pairing carries over. You only need to register your digital key again.

## Remote control

**Can I start the vehicle from the app?**

Remote starting is not supported. Instead, you can bring the cabin to temperature before you leave, with [Set the cabin temperature before you get in](./remote-control.md#set-the-cabin-temperature-before-you-get-in).

**Can I control the vehicle from far away?**

Yes. There is no distance limit, as long as the vehicle can get a signal. Unlocking the doors with your digital key, though, works only near the vehicle.

**Why does cabin temperature control switch off after 30 minutes?**

This is normal. It is a limit that protects the battery. Start it again if you need to.

## Software updates

**Can I decline an update?**

A standard update can be deferred. A required update, related to safety or security, installs automatically once a set period has passed.

**What if I need the vehicle during an update?**

Cancel the schedule if the installation has not started. Once it has started you have to wait for it to finish, which usually takes 15 to 30 minutes.

**Does this use my data allowance?**

No. The vehicle communicates over its own built-in connection. It does not use your phone's data.

## Battery and charging

**Why did my battery level drop suddenly?**

Battery performance falls temporarily in cold weather. The figure returns as the temperature recovers.

**Why does charging start immediately when I set a schedule?**

Either **Charge now** was selected just after the cable was connected, or the level is below the minimum threshold, in which case charging starts regardless of the schedule.

**What is the 12V auxiliary battery?**

It is a small battery, separate from the drive battery, that powers the vehicle's electronics. If it goes flat, the app and the vehicle cannot communicate.

## Digital key

**Can I unlock the doors if my phone runs out of power?**

It works for up to 6 hours after the phone powers off. After that you need the physical smart key.

**Can I see where someone I shared with has driven?**

No. Routes are not shared. Only unlock and start events are recorded, in [Check the sharing history](./digital-key.md#check-the-sharing-history).

## Data

**Is my location recorded continuously?**

It is recorded only while driving, and when you switch the vehicle off only the last parking position is kept. For detail, see [Manage your data and privacy](./privacy.md).

**Can I delete the records?**

Yes. You can delete your driving history under **Settings > Privacy > Delete data**.
