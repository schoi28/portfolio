# Check when service is due

Your vehicle tells you when it needs attention. The alerts are calculated from your mileage and how you use the vehicle.

## Service alerts

| Item | Based on | When you are alerted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tyre rotation | Every 10,000 km | 1,000 km before |
| Air conditioning filter | 12 months or 15,000 km | 2 weeks before |
| Brake pad check | Wear level | At 20% remaining or below |
| Coolant check | 24 months | 1 month before |
| 12V auxiliary battery | Health level | When replacement is recommended |

Selecting an alert shows what needs doing and your nearest service centre.

### Defer an alert

If it is awkward to deal with now, you can defer the alert.

1. Select the alert.
2. Select **Remind me later**.
3. Choose a period. You can pick 1 week, 2 weeks, or 1 month.

For items that bear directly on safety, such as the brake pads, the longest deferral is 1 week.

## Why a sensor cleaning alert appears

Your vehicle has cameras and a LiDAR sensor that support driving. Dust or water on a lens lowers detection performance, so the vehicle notices and tells you.

| Alert | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Check the front camera | The windscreen camera's view is obstructed |
| Sensor cleaning needed | Debris detected on the roof sensor surface |
| Sensor check needed | Detection has not recovered after cleaning |

### Clean the sensors yourself

:::warning[Warning]
Do not use a cleaner containing an organic solvent or alcohol. Damage to the lens coating means replacement. Avoid directing a pressure washer at a sensor as well.
:::

1. Switch the vehicle off.
2. Wipe the lens surface lightly with a soft dry cloth.
3. Check that the alert clears in the app. It can take up to 10 minutes.

If the alert does not clear after cleaning, the problem may be with the sensor itself. Contact a service centre.

## Book a service centre

1. Select **Book a service** in the alert, or go to **Settings > Service centres**.
2. Select a nearby centre.
3. Select a date and time.
4. Check the reason for the visit and select **Book**.

Your vehicle's recent diagnostic records are sent with the booking. The centre can prepare in advance, which shortens your visit.

### Change or cancel a booking

You can change or cancel under **Settings > Service centres > My bookings**, up to 24 hours before the visit.

## Change your alert settings

You can switch each item on or off under **Settings > Alerts**.

| Alert type | Default | Can it be switched off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Software updates | On | Yes |
| Service due | On | Yes |
| Sensor cleaning | On | Yes |
| Charging complete | On | Yes |
| Door opened | Off | Yes |
| Safety warnings | On | No |
