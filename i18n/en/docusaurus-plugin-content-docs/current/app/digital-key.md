# Manage your digital key

A digital key **turns your phone into your car key.** With your phone on you, taking hold of the handle unlocks the door, and once you are in, pressing the start button starts the vehicle. You can use the vehicle without the physical smart key, and you can send a key to family or a friend.

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Keys you can create for one vehicle | **Up to 5**, including the owner's |
| Devices per person | 1 |
| Resharing by someone you shared with | Not possible |
| After the phone runs out of power | Works for up to 6 hours |

There is **no limit on how many vehicles** you add to one account. The 5 is the number of keys you can create for a single vehicle.

## Check this before you use it

A digital key works on a signal exchanged directly between your phone and the vehicle when the two are close together. So, separately from your internet connection, **two settings on the phone** have to be on.

| Setting | Why it is needed |
| --- | --- |
| **Bluetooth** | The channel through which your phone and the vehicle recognise each other. With it off, taking hold of the handle does not unlock the door |
| **Location permission, set to always allow** | It works out whether your phone is near the vehicle. Set to "while using the app", it does not work once the app is closed |

:::info[Caution]
Below 5% battery, power saving on some phones switches Bluetooth off. Use the physical smart key then.
:::

:::note[Note]
With all 5 keys created, you have to revoke an existing key before you can share a new one. How to revoke one is in [Manage and revoke shared permissions](#manage-and-revoke-shared-permissions).
:::

## Register your digital key

Do this inside the vehicle.

1. Switch Bluetooth on, and set the app's location permission to **Always allow**.
2. Get in and start the vehicle.
3. In the app, select **Digital key > Register key**.
4. Place your phone on the vehicle's wireless charging pad.
5. Enter the six-digit code shown on the vehicle screen into the app.
6. A key icon appears in the app once registration finishes.

Registration takes about a minute. Do not take your phone off the pad during that time.

### How to use it

| Action | How |
| --- | --- |
| Unlock the doors | With your phone on you, take hold of the driver's door handle |
| Start the vehicle | Get in and press the start button |
| Lock the doors | After getting out, press the lock button on the handle |

A digital key keeps working for up to 6 hours after your phone runs out of power. The physical smart key continues to work as normal.

## Share a key with family

![The four stages of sharing a key](/img/app-key-sharing.en.svg)

:::info[Caution]
An invitation expires **72 hours** after you send it. Once it expires you have to send it again.
:::

1. Select **Digital key > Share key**.
2. Enter the recipient's email address.
3. Select the permission level.
4. Select **Send invitation**.

The recipient selects the link in the invitation email, installs the app, and accepts with their own account. They can use the key as soon as they accept.

### Permission levels

| Permission | What they can do | What they cannot do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full | Lock and unlock, start, remote control, drive | Share keys, change the owner |
| Driving | Lock and unlock, start, drive | Remote control, share keys |
| Access | Lock and unlock only | Start, drive |

Use **Access** when someone only needs to get into the vehicle, for a delivery or a car wash.

### Limit how long it lasts

You can set a period when you select the permission level.

1. On the permission screen, switch **Limit period** on.
2. Set a start date and an end date.

The key is deactivated automatically once the end date passes. You do not have to revoke it separately.

## Manage and revoke shared permissions

:::warning[Warning]
If the vehicle is parked, revoking a key takes effect at once. If the other person is driving, it takes effect from the next start. To restore access, share the key again.
:::

1. Select **Digital key > Shared keys**.
2. Select the person from the list.
3. Change their permission, or select **Revoke key**.

### Check the sharing history

**Digital key > Usage history** shows who used the vehicle and when. The history is kept for 90 days.

| What is recorded | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Unlock | 4 March 18:22, Gildong Hong |
| Start | 4 March 18:24, Gildong Hong |

## If you change or lose your phone

| Situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Changing phone | Install the app on the new phone and register again. The old key is released automatically |
| Losing your phone | Sign in on another device and select **Digital key > Release key on this device** |
| Suspecting your account password has leaked | Changing the password releases every digital key. You have to register again |
