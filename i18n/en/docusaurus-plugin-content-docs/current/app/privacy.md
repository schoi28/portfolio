# Manage your data and privacy

This chapter explains what data your vehicle collects, what it is used for, and how you can manage it.

## Data that is collected

| Type | Examples | When it is collected | Retention |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vehicle state | Battery level, tyre pressures, door state | Continuously | 90 days |
| Driving history | Distance, time, efficiency | While driving | 12 months |
| Location | Route driven, last parking position | While driving | 30 days |
| Diagnostic information | Error codes, component state | When a fault occurs | 24 months |
| App usage | The history of remote commands | When a command runs | 90 days |

Data is deleted automatically once its retention period passes.

## What the data is used for

| Purpose | Data used |
| --- | --- |
| Providing app features | Vehicle state, location |
| Telling you when service is due | Driving history, diagnostic information |
| Improving the software | Diagnostic information, with identifying details removed |
| Investigating faults | Diagnostic information |

Routes and location data are not used for advertising and are not passed to third parties.

## Manage your data

### Adjust what is collected

You can adjust each item under **Settings > Privacy > Data collection**.

| Item | Can it be switched off | Features limited if you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Location | Yes | Find vehicle, routes driven |
| Driving history | Yes | Driving report |
| Diagnostic information | No | Not applicable. It is required for safety |
| Taking part in software improvement | Yes | None |

:::note[Note]
Diagnostic information bears directly on safety, so its collection cannot be stopped. It is, however, handled separately from information that identifies your vehicle.
:::

### Download your data

Under **Settings > Privacy > Download my data** you can receive the collected data as a file. It takes up to 7 days from the request, and you are notified when it is ready.

### Delete your data

Select what to delete under **Settings > Privacy > Delete data**.

:::warning[Warning]
Deleted data cannot be recovered. Your driving report and usage history go with it.
:::

Diagnostic information that must be retained by law may be kept for a period even after a deletion request.

## When you pass the vehicle on

Always do the following before selling or returning the vehicle.

1. Revoke every shared key under **Settings > Digital key**.
2. Delete your driving history and location under **Settings > Privacy > Delete data**.
3. Select **Settings > Vehicle > Unpair**.
4. Carry out a reset on the vehicle screen as well. This clears the favourites and paired phone details stored in the vehicle.

Unpairing removes the data from the app immediately, but what remains in the vehicle itself is only cleared by step 4.

## Data belonging to users you shared with

Data belonging to someone you shared a digital key with is handled as follows.

| Item | Visible to the owner |
| --- | --- |
| Unlock and start times | Yes |
| The person's name | Yes |
| Routes driven | No |
| Driving report | No |

## Getting in touch

For questions about how personal data is handled, use **Settings > Privacy > Contact us**.
