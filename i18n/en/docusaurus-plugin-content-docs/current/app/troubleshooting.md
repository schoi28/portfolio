# Troubleshooting

What to check for each symptom. Work down the list in order.

## My vehicle will not connect

The most common cause is the vehicle being somewhere it cannot get a signal.

| Check | How | If that does not fix it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where it is parked | In an underground car park, move above ground and check again | Go to the next item |
| Last communication time | Check it on the home screen. 30 minutes or more means a problem on the vehicle | Go to the next item |
| Auxiliary battery | Switch the vehicle on and charge for at least 10 minutes | Go to the next item |
| Restart the app | Close the app completely and open it again | Go to the next item |
| Pair again | **Settings > Vehicle > Unpair**, then pair again | Contact a service centre |

:::note[Note]
If the vehicle is not driven for 2 weeks or more, it communicates less often to protect the battery. The app can then be up to 30 minutes behind, which is not a fault.
:::

## A remote command will not run

| Symptom | Cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Cannot reach your vehicle" | It is somewhere with no signal | Work through the section above first |
| "Your vehicle is not responding" | The auxiliary battery is low | Switch the vehicle on to charge it |
| "The previous command is still running" | Commands overlapped | Try again after 30 seconds |
| "Check your vehicle's state" | A precondition is unmet | See the table below |
| It waits with no response | A software installation is under way | Wait until the installation finishes |

### Preconditions by command

| Command | State required |
| --- | --- |
| Cabin temperature | Parked |
| Start charging | The charging cable is connected |
| Start the vehicle with a digital key | Near the vehicle, with the digital key registered |
| Every command | Auxiliary battery at 20% or above |

## My update failed

If an installation fails, the vehicle returns to the previous version on its own. You can use the vehicle as normal.

| Alert message | Cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Battery too low | Below 30% | Charge and try again |
| Check your vehicle's state | The vehicle was not parked | Put the gear in P and try again |
| The installation was interrupted | The vehicle was started during the installation | Try again |
| The installation failed | Unknown | Contact a service centre if it happens 3 times |

If the same update fails 3 times in a row, automatic retries stop and the app shows directions to a service centre.

## I am not receiving alerts

1. In your phone settings, check that notifications are allowed for VELA Drive.
2. In the app, check that the item is on under **Settings > Alerts**.
3. Check whether your phone's power saving or do not disturb mode is on.
4. Sign out of the app and sign in again.

On Android, alerts can be delayed if the app is closed in the background. Exclude VELA Drive from battery optimisation.

## My digital key is not recognised

| Check | How |
| --- | --- |
| Bluetooth | Check that Bluetooth is on |
| Location permission | Check the app's location permission is set to "Always allow" |
| Battery | It may not work below 5% phone battery |
| Key state | On the **Digital key** screen, check the key is active |
| Register again | Release the key and register it again inside the vehicle |

The physical smart key always works, regardless of the digital key.

## My range does not match reality

Range is an estimate. It varies with the following.

- Battery efficiency falls at low outside temperatures.
- A lot of high-speed driving worsens efficiency.
- Heavy use of the air conditioning or heater increases consumption.

It is continually corrected from your recent driving, so if your driving changes, the figure adjusts over several days.

## The app is slow or frozen

1. Close the app completely and open it again.
2. Update the app to the latest version.
3. Restart your phone.
4. Delete the app and install it again. Your vehicle pairing and digital key survive a reinstall.

## If none of this helps

You can get in touch under **Settings > Contact us**, attaching diagnostic information. That information covers your app version, your vehicle's software version, and recent error records. It does not include your location or your driving history.
