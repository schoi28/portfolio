# About Me

I am a Korean–English technical writer with degrees in creative writing and computer science. I grew up in Korea and earned degrees in both Korea and the United States. I write directly in both Korean and English, and translate between them when a document calls for it.

What I do best is spot where documentation is needed and take it from planning to a finished set. I gather what I need by working with engineering, QA, and product teams, and build documents that readers can follow and use in their work. Learning a new technology or domain and setting down what I learned as clear documentation matters to me too.

The problems I have found and how I solved them are in [Portfolio](/projects). The various document samples built around the fictional VELA product family are in [Sample Docs](/samples), and my experience and skills are in [Resume](/resume).
